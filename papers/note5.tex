Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $N := \{1,\ldots,n\}$.
We identify each subset of $N$ with the strictly increasing sequence
of its elements.
Let $G$ be a subgroup of the symmetric group $S_n$.
We define a total order $<_{l}$ on $2^N$ by declaring that $a <_{l} a'$ if and only if
\[
|a| < |a'|,
\quad\text{or}\quad
\bigl(|a| = |a'| \text{ and } a <_{\mathrm{lex}} a'\bigr),
\]
where $<_{\mathrm{lex}}$ denotes the lexicographic order.
We say that $a \in 2^N$ is \emph{$G$-minimal} if it is the least
element of its orbit $a^G$.
If $a$ is $G$-minimal, then every prefix of $a$ is also $G$-minimal.

Let $A$ be a finite abelian group of size $n$.
Fix a bijection $A \cong N$, and use it to equip $2^A$ with the
total order defined above.
Let $G$ be a subgroup of $\operatorname{Aut}(A)$, viewed as a
subgroup of $S_n$ via this bijection.
Let $P$ be a property of subgroups of $A$ that is $G$-invariant
and preserved under taking subgroups.
A subgroup of $A$ satisfying $P$ is called a $P$-subgroup.
Our aim is to list all  $P$-subgroups of $A$  modulo the action of $G$.
For a $P$-subgroup $B$,
 let $\mingen(B)$ be the $<_{l}$-minimal  generating set  of  $B$.
 We define a total order $<_{\beta}$ on the set of all  $P$-subgroups of $A$ 
 by 
 \[
 B<_{\beta}B\sprime\;\;  \Longleftrightarrow \;\;\mingen(B)<_{l} \mingen(B\sprime).
 \]
A $P$-subgroup $B$ is said to be $G$-minimal 
if $B$ is $<_{\beta}$-minimal  in 
 the $G$-orbit $B^G$ of $B$ by $G$.
For every $G$-minimal $P$-subgroup $B\ne \{0\}$,
there exists   a  strictly  increasing sequence $[g_1, \dots, g_m]$  (where we use the order on $A$ by the bijection $A \cong N$) such that 
\[
0\;\subsetneq\; \angs{g_1}\;\subsetneq\; \angs{g_1, g_2}\;\subsetneq\; \dots \;\subsetneq\; \angs{g_1, \dots, g_m}=B
\]
and that $\{g_1, \dots, g_m\}$ is $G$-minimal.
