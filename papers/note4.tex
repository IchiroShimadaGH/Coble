Let $k$ be a positive integer.
We consider the complete graph with vertex set $V_k=\{1, \dots, k\}$ and edge set 
\[
E_k:=\set {[i, j]}{i, j\in V_k, i<j}.
\]
Let $m$ be a positive integer, and we put 
\[
M:=\{-m,-m+1, \dots, m\}.
\]
Let $\mathcal{W}_k$ be the set of weights $w \colon E_k \to M$, 
that is, $\mathcal{W}_k$ is the set of weighted complete graphs on $V_k$.
Then  the symmetric group $\SSSS_k$ act on $V_k$ from the right.
Then $\SSS_k$ acts on $\mathcal{W}_k$ from theleft.
We also define $\SW_k$ to be the multiplicative group of all mappings
$s \colon V_k \to \{\pm 1\}$, 
which acts on $\mathcal{W}_k$ by switching, 
that is, 
\[
(sw) ([i, j])= s(i) s(j)  w([i, j])
\]
for $s\in \SW_k$.
Then the semidirect product
\[
G_k := \SW_k \rtimes \SSSS_k
\]
acts on $\mathcal{W}_k$ from the left.
The purpose of this section is to describe an efficient algorithm
for obtaining the list of 
orbits of the action of $G_k$ on $\mathcal{W}_k$.

First, we introduce a total order on the set $E_k$ of edges by
\[
[i,j] < [i',j']
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
j < j'
\quad\text{or}\quad
(j = j' \text{ and } i < i').
\]
Then we express elements $w$ of $\mathcal{W}_k$ by arranging
the values $w([i,j])$ in this order:
\[
w = \bigl(w([1,2]), w([1,3]), w([2,3]), w([1,4]), \ldots \bigr).
\]
Let $|w|$ denote the vector obtained from $w$ by taking
the absolute value of each component.
We put $|M| := \{0,1,\ldots,m\}$.
Then we have a mapping
%
\begin{equation}\label{eq:absw}
\mathcal{W}_k \longrightarrow |\mathcal{W}_k| := |M|^{E_k},
\qquad
w \longmapsto |w|.
\end{equation}
%
Notice that $\SSSS_k$ acts on $|\mathcal{W}_k|$, and the mapping~\eqref{eq:absw}
is $\SSSS_k$-equivariant.
Moreover, the action of $\SW_k$ on $\mathcal{W}_k$ preserves
each fiber of~\eqref{eq:absw}.
We then define a total order on $\mathcal{W}_k$ by
\[
w < w'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
|w| <_{\mathrm{lex}} |w'|
\quad\text{or}\quad
\bigl(|w| = |w'| \text{ and } w <_{\mathrm{lex}} w'\bigr).
\]
We say that $w\in\mathcal{W}_k $ is $G_k$-minimal if $w$ is minimal
in its orbit $wG_k$.
We obtain the list of $G_k$-orbits in $\mathcal{W}_k$
by enumerating all $G_k$-minimal elements in $\mathcal{W}_k$.
By definition, we have the following:
%$w$ is $G_k$-minimal if and only if $|w|$ is minimal
%in its $G_k$-orbit $G_k |w|$ in $|\Wk|$, and $w$ is minimal
%in its $\SW_k$-orbit  $\SW_k w$.
\[
w \text{ is } G_k\text{-minimal}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\parbox{8cm}{
$|w|$ is minimal with respect to $<_{\mathrm{lex}}$ in its $\SSSS_k$-orbit 
$\SSSS_k |w|$ in $|\Wk|$, and 
$w$  is minimal  with respect to $<_{\mathrm{lex}}$ in its $ \SW_k$-orbit $\SW_k w$.}
\]
By this observation, to determine whether an element
$w \in \Wk$ is $G_k$-minimal, it suffices to make at most
$k! + 2^k$ comparisons, which is much smaller than the size
$2^k k!$ of $G_k$.
\par
Suppose that $k' < k$.
By taking the prefix of length $k'(k'-1)/2$ of each $w \in \Wk$,
we obtain a mapping
\[
\Wk \longrightarrow \mathcal{W}_{k'},
\qquad
w \longmapsto w|_{k'}.
\]
%
\begin{lemma}
Suppose that $w \in \Wk$ is $G_k$-minimal.
Then $w|_{k'}$ is $G_{k'}$-minimal.
\end{lemma}
%
\begin{proof}
We have a natural embedding $G_{k'} \hookrightarrow G_k$
denoted by $g' \mapsto \widetilde{g}\sprime$
by 
\[
i\sp{\widetilde{\tau}\sprime}:=\begin{cases}
i^{\tau\sprime} & \textrm{if $i\le k\sprime$,}
\\
i & \textrm{if $i> k\sprime$,}
\end{cases}
\qquad
\widetilde{s}\sprime(i):=
\begin{cases}
s\sprime(i) & \textrm{if $i\le k\sprime$,}
\\
i & \textrm{if $i> k\sprime$,}
\end{cases}
\]
for $\tau\sprime\in \SSSS_{k\sprime}$ and $s\sprime \in\SW_{k\sprime}$.
\end{proof}
%
Using this lemma, we construct the list of $G_k$-minimal elements
by induction on $k$.
